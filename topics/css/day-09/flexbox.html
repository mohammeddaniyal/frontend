Quick mental model

  • display: flex — arrange direct children with Flexbox.
  • flex-direction — choose row or column.
  • justify-content — arrange items along the main axis.
  • align-items — align items along the cross axis.
  • gap — add space between items.
  • flex-wrap — allow items to move onto another line.
